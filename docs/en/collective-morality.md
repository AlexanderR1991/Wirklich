# Collective morality · A revisable direction index

**REALLY? / WIRKLICH · research proposal KM-0.1 · 7 November 2026 · unvalidated translation draft**

> We do not declare good and bad once and for all. We build a process in which justified moral directions can emerge, be challenged and change.

**Research question:** Can independently examined effects, genuinely diverse human perspectives and documented objections support collectively developing moral orientation? **Hypothesis:** In *carefully delimited contexts*, a numeric index might eventually be justified. More data and participants alone neither create moral obligations nor overcome biased sampling or value conflict. **There is no operational or validated scoring system today.**

This proposal complements, but does not replace, [META-01](../selbstkorrektur.md), [maturity R0–R4](../erkenntnisreife.md), [human governance](../community-governance.md) and [the AI charter](../ki-charta.md).

## Keep the dimensions separate

| Axis | Question | Never confuse with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence | How well are facts and consequences supported? | moral goodness |
| Value profile | What goals, rights and priorities are assumed? | an objective fact found in data |
| Effect | How does one option compare with a specified alternative? | an unexamined causal claim |
| Direction | How does an option fare within a stated value profile? | truth or a person's worth |
| Consensus / dissent | Who shares which reasons and with what limits? | proof or popularity |

Maturity, abstraction, claim type, evidence, consensus and the proposed direction remain independent. A positive direction cannot promote a claim automatically.

## From a direction map to an optional number

Start with an inspectable **direction map**: the precise option, the comparator, affected people, time horizon, direct and indirect effects, sources, strong objections, multiple value profiles, uncertainties and missing perspectives. **Not assessed** is not neutral and never equals zero.

Only with an adequately justified comparison might a **−1 to +1 conditional index** follow: negative and positive means harm or benefit under the declared scale, reference and priorities; zero means a *supported approximate balance*, not ignorance. It is not a probability, a certificate of objective morality or a rating of people.

One *falsifiable candidate model* is:

\[
D(a \mid z,c,E)=\sum_{j=1}^{n}w_j(z)d_j(a \mid c,E)
\]

In words: add the effects **d** of option **a**, estimated for each dimension **j** from evaluated evidence **E** and context **c**, using publicly declared non-negative value weights **w** (summing to one) for profile **z**. This requires commensurable and justified scales, avoids double-counting dependent evidence and does **not** derive normative weights from facts. It is a research proposal, not the mathematics of objective morality.

Any future scalar must include the reference, all impact axes, weights, competing profiles, error range, source and algorithm versions, strongest surviving objection and responsible human review. The decimal precision of +0.475 is **not earned** merely by displaying it.

## All gates before any public number

1. Specify the action, comparator, scope, outcomes, affected people and possible bias.
2. Pre-register measures, reference anchors, exclusions, value profiles, data sources and failure conditions.
3. Independently review relevant effect assumptions to **at least R3** under the *existing* maturity rules. Neither AI nor popularity can promote them.
4. Deliberately seek affected and poorly represented perspectives; preserve minority reasons. Do not give popularity epistemic weight.
5. Test calibration on separate cases, dependencies, sampling errors and uncertainty intervals. Do not fabricate AI confidence.
6. Stress-test alternate plausible references, values and data. If the sign reverses, publish **multiple profiles, ranges or unresolved disagreement**, not a universal single number.
7. Examine dignity, rights, serious harm, discrimination, law and abuse independently, then require accountable human editorial approval under existing governance.

No magic sample size unlocks moral truth. A field-specific release and calibration plan must itself be contestable and independently assessed. Protected rights cannot be quietly traded away by an aggregate formula.

## Two everyday stress tests

**“Vegetarian diets are good.”** For whom? Compared with which diet? Under what nutritional, ecological, animal-welfare, cost and cultural conditions? **No numeric score** until the question and value profiles are adequately defined.

**“Buying Gut & Günstig dog food.”** Which product and dog, nutritional needs, alternatives, sourcing and affordability? Calling it −0.014 would be invented. **Not scored** is neither a bad nor a neutral verdict.

**Strongest objection:** A homogeneous, privileged majority could manufacture “collective” morality and overlook people living with the consequences. A well-grounded minority objection must be able to pause review or challenge weights even against enormous popularity. An unsupported objection is not an automatic truth veto.

## Roles, convergence and corrections

**Humans** supply experience, normative premises, evidence, counterarguments and accountable decisions; none of these is interchangeable with vote counts. Participation must remain voluntary, accessible and sensitive to those who cannot join. **AI** may separate facts and values, identify missed stakeholders, propose comparisons, trace sources and test assumptions. It has no human vote, automatic review authority or permission to publish moral scores.

A **convergence layer** explores which *conditional* directions remain stable across independently defined communities, fields and value profiles. Convergence generates new questions to test, never a final universal rule.

Every revision must preserve a stable ID, previous output, assumptions and weights, sources, strongest objection, dated human decision and replacement or withdrawal. Strong new evidence can **suspend, narrow, revise or withdraw** a number. No numeric result is permanent.

**Forbidden:** ratings of persons or groups, social-credit systems, automated sanctions, covert persuasion, replacement of democratic decisions or professional high-stakes medical/legal judgment. Stress-test bot swarms, Sybil attacks, bought inputs, dependent sources, exclusion and Goodhart-style metric gaming.

## Research phases

**KM-0 now:** bilingual proposal, machine-readable draft and stress cases with **score = null**, no live computation or data collection.

**KM-1:** pre-register one narrow action, comparator, affected populations, outcomes and value profiles.

**KM-2:** independently examine evidence; test the non-numeric direction map before any supervised numeric model.

**KM-3:** blind tests with diverse independent reviewers on calibration, robustness, harms, legibility and **map versus score** comparisons; report failures.

**KM-4:** if every gate is satisfied, consider provisional, versioned public numbers subject to human approval and meaningful appeal. Otherwise keep the direction map or open disagreement.

**Stop** if comparison, calibration, representation, protection of rights or robustness is missing—or the score performs worse than an unscored map.

[Social choice theory](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/social-choice/) shows the inherent limits of aggregating individual judgments. We do not promise one coherent moral ordering for all people. We investigate when **conditional and corrigible common orientation** is genuinely useful.
